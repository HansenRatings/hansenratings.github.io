Glossary of Terms

Team Metrics

AdjEff
Opponent-Adjusted Efficiency - A team's projected margin of victory against an average opponent on a neutral field
AdjO
Opponent-Adjusted Offensive Efficiency - A team's projected points for against an average team on a neutral field
AdjD
Opponent-Adjusted Defensive Efficiency - A team's projected points allowed against an average team on a neutral field
AdjHFA
Opponent-Adjusted Home-Field Advantage - The expected positive/negative difference in team performance from a neutral field to home/away
Typical Top 25 Team
A hypothetical team with an AdjEff rating of +30
Elite Team
A hypothetical team with an AdjEff rating of +36
Good Team
A hypothetical team with an AdjEff rating of +18
Average Team
A hypothetical team with an AdjEff rating of 0
MOV+
Margin of Victory Plus - How much more or less you’ve outscored your opponents than a typical Top 25 team would be expected to
SOR
Strength of Record - The likelihood a typical Top 25 team would have a W-L record at least as good against the same opponents
E+
Elite Games - The percentage of games where a team performed at an elite level or better
G+
Good Games - The percentage of games where a team performed at a good level or better
A+
Average Games - The percentage of games where a team performed at an average level or better
BA
Below Average Games - The percentage of games where a team performed at a below average level
EAW
Elite Adjusted Wins - How many more/fewer games the team has won than an elite team would be expected to win
GAW
Good Adjusted Wins - How many more/fewer games the team has won than a good team would be expected to win
AAW
Average Adjusted Wins - How many more/fewer games the team has won than an average team would be expected to win
Pool A
The pool of playoff bids available to conferences' champions
Pool B
The pool of playoff bids available to independent teams or teams in a conference that does not have an automatic bid (every DIII football conference currently has an automatic bid, and not enough teams are independent to qualify for any Pool B bids)
Pool C
The pool of playoff bids available for teams who did not receive a Pool A or Pool B bid
SOS
Strength of Schedule - The average expected win probability of a team's opponents against an average team
SOS+
Strength of Schedule Plus - The average expected win probability of a team's opponents against a typical Top 25 team
NCSOS
Non-Conference Strength of Schedule - The average expected win probability of a team's non-conference opponents against an average team

Player Metrics

TAY/P
Total Adjusted Yards per Play - An efficiency metric that gives boosts for positive plays and penalties for negative plays:
pTAYP
Playmaker Total Adjusted Yards per Play - For defenders, the average yards prevented due to the player’s box score statistics
dTAYP
Defender Total Adjusted Yards per Play - For defenders, the average yards prevented/allowed due to their non-box score contributions to the overall team defensive efficiency
TAY/P+
Total Adjusted Yards per Play Plus - Opponent-adjusted total adjusted yards per play
HVC
Havoc Plays - Tackles for loss, pass breakups, interceptions, forced fumbles, fumble recoveries, and blocked kicks/punts
YARP
Yards Above Replacement Player - How many yards an offensive player added/a defensive player prevented relative to what a replacement player would have accounted for
Replacement Player
A hypothetical player who has the average production and efficiency of all non-starters at his position
